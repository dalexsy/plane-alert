View Cone Configuration

Configure the viewing angles and labels for your location. Each cone represents a direction you can see planes from.

Angles: 0° = North, 90° = East, 180° = South, 270° = West

Tip: Most buildings have 4 sides. Consider 90° arcs for each direction.

Note: Configured view cones are only shown when you're at your home location. At other locations, full 360° circular bands are displayed instead.
